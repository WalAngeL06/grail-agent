# GRAIL 30-day replay

In a 30-day simulation on real OKX hourly prices (Sep 17, 2026 to Oct 17, 2026) with no real orders sent, GRAIL turned 50.00 USDT into 51.01 USDT (+2.0%), while buying and holding all 5 coins equally ended at 54.41 USDT (+8.8%). Its worst drop from a high point was 4.3% versus 14.5% for buy-and-hold, and it moved fully to cash 11 times when prices turned down. On average the 5 coins behaved like only 1.4 independent bets because they tend to move together, so GRAIL sizes them as one group instead of treating each coin as a separate bet.
